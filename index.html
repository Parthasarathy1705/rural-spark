01 · The energy gap

The grid cannot reach these homes, and the off-grid fix is 12% done

India’s rural grid supplied 22.6 hours a day in 2025. For one lakh tribal and PVTG homes the grid is not feasible, so they are served off-grid. By 30 June 2026, 11,838 had been electrified.

04 · The innovation

Same panels, same battery: essential failures fall from 42 days a year to under one

Every hour the controller works out how much energy tonight’s essentials need, looks one day ahead using yesterday’s sun (so it works offline), and lets comfort and processing loads use only what is left. Tier 1 is never shed by software.

42.5days/yr, no priority rule
6.9days/yr, fixed battery floors
0.6days/yr, Rural Spark (offline)

08 · What the pack tells the business

The battery lasts long enough for the finance plan

With our controller the pack runs about 118 full cycles a year. It reaches 80% capacity in year 9.6 and about 73% in year 13. Essentials still hold at 100% with the battery at 70%, so the finance model replaces it in year 13 on measured health and tests year 10 as a downside.

What these results are not: loads are assumed schedules until a 4-week metered baseline; the models are energy models (no motor starting, faults or phase imbalance); PVGIS is satellite reanalysis, so on-site shading is not yet verified; no hardware has run in the field yet.
